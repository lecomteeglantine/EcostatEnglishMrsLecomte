The Hiring Committee

Three applicants want the same interview. Their CVs are strong. Their cover letters are not. Compare the letters, make six committee decisions, then choose who gets the interview.

3 or 4students
6 missionsone decision each

How to play

Read. Discuss. Decide.

1 · ReadRead the three short cover-letter extracts.
2 · DiscussEveryone gives one reason before the click.
3 · ChooseSelect the strongest candidate for that mission.
4 · BuildYour choice is added to the final pitch.

Same choice = same result. There is no random scoring, shuffled answer or AI judgement. Every device uses the same fixed rules.

Your final task

Choose one interview candidate.

Then defend the committee decision in one connected 2-minute team pitch.
