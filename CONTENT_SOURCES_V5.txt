V5 PROGRAMME ALIGNMENT

The V5 pathway vocabulary and training priorities were aligned with the current ISFA Master Econometrics & Statistics overview available in August 2026, including the subject areas listed for:
- EQUADE: statistics & AI, data mining/text mining/visualisation, surveys/data manipulation, econometric evaluation, applied econometrics/microeconomics and economic decision-making.
- DSI: data mining, data architectures, IT risk analysis, systems/networks risk, web services/security, cryptology and protection of computations.
- IRF: statistics & AI, technical/financial risk, stochastic calculus for finance, financial engineering and financial theory.
- GRAF: statistics & AI, game theory/economics, financial mathematics, risk management, life/non-life actuarial mathematics and credit risk.

Reference:
https://isfa.univ-lyon1.fr/formation/econometrie-statistiques
