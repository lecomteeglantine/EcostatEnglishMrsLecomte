About

What is inside?

The vocabulary base combines Mrs Lecomte's 2026 course corpus with terminology aligned to the EQUADE, DSI, IRF and GRAF programme content, plus a clearly labelled professional extension for workplace readiness.

Use the source filter in the dictionary to distinguish course vocabulary from programme and professional extensions.

Privacy · RGPD-friendly by design

No account, no analytics, no advertising tracker and no remote database. Favourites, mastery and quiz statistics are saved only in your browser's local storage.

If you clear browser storage, progress is erased unless you export a backup first.
