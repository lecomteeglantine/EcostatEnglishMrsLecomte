Beat the ATS

Your team must tailor one Ecostat candidate's CV or résumé for a specific vacancy. First survive the software scan. Then make the human recruiter keep reading. Ten locked decisions determine your final score.

Mission format
3–4students
20–25 mingame + preparation
10 rounds10 points each
2 min maxper speaker

Fair-score rule. Every option has a fixed value. The same candidate, the same decision and the same option always produce the same points and the same final result on every device. Option order is fixed A–D. No random bonuses, no shuffled answers and no AI scoring.

How to play

One vacancy. Two readers. One team decision.

1 · ReadStudy the vacancy, the candidate evidence and all four options.
2 · DecideDiscuss first. Once someone clicks, that answer is locked.
3 · DefendAfter round 10, everyone explains part of the final application.
Final presentation

Know exactly what happens after the game.

The website assigns each student a different speaking role after the score is revealed.

Do not write a full script. You get six minutes for short notes. At 2:00, finish your sentence and stop.
