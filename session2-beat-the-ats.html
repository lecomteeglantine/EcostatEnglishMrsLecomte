Beat the ATS

Your team must tailor one Ecostat candidate's CV or résumé for a specific vacancy. First survive the software scan. Then make the human recruiter keep reading. Ten locked decisions determine your final score — and each decision also builds your final team pitch.

Mission format
3 or 4students
20–25 mingame + rehearsal
10 rounds10 points each
2:00 maxfor the whole team pitch

Fair-score rule. Every option has a fixed value. The same candidate, decision and option always produce the same points and final result on every device. No random bonuses, shuffled answers or AI scoring.

How to play

One vacancy. Two readers. One team decision.

1 · Read & discussStudy the vacancy, candidate evidence and all four options before anyone clicks.
2 · Lock one choiceOnce selected, the answer is fixed. The website immediately adds it to your pitch builder.
3 · Build as you goEach round tells one student what that choice contributes to their part of the final pitch.
Final team pitch

Two minutes total — everyone speaks.

You are not preparing four separate mini-presentations. You are building one short, connected team pitch.

3 students40 seconds each = 2:00 total.
4 students30 seconds each = 2:00 total.
Use your real decisionsExplain what you chose and why it improves ATS fit or recruiter impact.
Prepare throughoutThe pitch builder assigns every round to a speaker, so the final rehearsal is only for linking and timing.

Keep it concise. Use short notes, not a full script. The final presentation has one shared 2-minute timer for the whole team.
